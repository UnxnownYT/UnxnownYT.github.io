Liquix

A minimal Chromium browser for Mac, built with SwiftUI and Liquid Glass. It looks and feels like a native macOS 26 app, while every website runs on the same engine as Chrome.

What's inside

  • Liquid Glass tab barSplit view, drag tabs out into new windows, and a ⌃⇥ tab switcher.
  • Spotlight-style searchPress ⌘T to search or jump to a site.
  • Start pageA clock, the weather and your own backgrounds.
  • Built-in ad blockerUses uBlock Origin filter lists, with nothing extra to install.
  • History, downloads and bookmarksAll built in and stored on your Mac.
  • Make it yoursCustomise shortcuts, animations, window glass, tint and corner rounding.
